# Demo questions

1. **Data Analyst:** How did delivered net product sales and delivered order count change in September 2025 versus August 2025 and September 2024? Show formulas and source evidence.
2. **Customer Analyst:** Did the September 2025 change differ between first-time and repeat customers under the stated window definition?
3. **Product & Operations:** Which categories and SKUs had the largest sales change, and what do the September inventory snapshots show?
4. **Forecasting:** Forecast delivered net product sales for the next three months using a stated method and uncertainty. Explain why two synthetic years provide limited confidence.
5. **Verification:** Recalculate one reported sales change from order/item rows; flag any unsupported cause statement.
6. **Cross-agent:** Did the delivery delay or return pattern change during September 2025? Report associations as observations, not causes.
## Customer Analyst

- Compare delivered customer lifecycle metrics in September 2025, August 2025, and September 2024, including active customers, new versus returning status, repeat-customer rate, purchase frequency, and net product sales per active customer.
- For recorded first-order cohorts in September 2025, August 2025, and September 2024, show monthly delivered-order retention from M+0 through M+3 and mark offsets beyond the observed order-date range unavailable.
- Compare active customers, delivered orders, repeat-customer rate, and net product sales per active customer in September 2025, August 2025, and September 2024 by customer segment, customer region, and acquisition channel.
